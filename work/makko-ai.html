The company

What Makko AI is

Makko AI is an AI-powered 2D game studio SaaS. The pitch is direct: remove the hardest barriers in game development so anyone with an idea can ship a game. No art skills required. No coding required.

The company was rebranded from Snib.ai to Makko AI in 2025. I led the rebrand end-to-end: naming, positioning, messaging architecture, and a lightweight brand system, all without pausing product velocity.

The founding team is four people: Jeremy Bird (Co-Founder, CEO and CTO), Anthony "Tony" Valcarcel (Co-Founder, Investor Relations), Mike Fehlauer Hayes (Co-Founder, helped create PAX and former VP of Penny Arcade), and me (Co-Founder, Director of Operations).

Origin

The prototype I co-built with Tony

Before Makko AI had a name, a CEO, or a team, it was a two-person prototype. Tony Valcarcel and I built the original Makko AI prototype together. That working prototype is what attracted Jeremy Bird (now CEO), Mike Fehlauer Hayes, and the rest of the founding team. The prototype became the company.

I was there from the first commit. Most "founder" stories rewrite the early history. This is mine: I helped build the thing before the thing existed.

Decisions

Specific calls I made at Makko

Co-built the original Makko AI prototype with Tony Valcarcel

The two-person prototype is what attracted the rest of the founding team. The prototype became the company. I was there from the first commit.

Position Makko AI as art-first, not game-builder-first

User analytics showed 70% of incoming users came for the art generation pipeline. Repositioned external messaging, landing page hierarchy, and content calendar around art-first within a single week. Bounce rate dropped, time-on-page rose.

Led the rebrand from Snib.ai to Makko AI

Owned naming, positioning, messaging architecture, and the brand system. Maintained full product velocity during the transition. No pause in shipping, no team disruption.

Migrated the blog from subdomain to root domain

Subdomain blogs accrue authority separately from the main domain. With Makko at DR 12, splitting authority between two surfaces was the wrong trade. Consolidated to makko.ai/blog, redirected legacy URLs with 301s, centralized link equity on one domain.

Built the UTM registry at go.makko.ai before scaling distribution

Untracked links lose campaign attribution forever. Stood up a UTM short-link domain with a documented naming convention before publishing the first batch of cross-platform content. Every external link is now attributable to campaign, channel, and content piece.
