The company

What Makko AI is

Makko AI is an AI-powered 2D game studio SaaS. The pitch is direct: remove the hardest barriers in game development so anyone with an idea can ship a game. No art skills required. No coding required.

The company was rebranded from Snib.ai to Makko AI in 2025. I led the rebrand end-to-end: naming, positioning, messaging architecture, and a lightweight brand system, all without pausing product velocity.

The founding team is four people: Jeremy Bird (Co-Founder, CEO and CTO), Anthony "Tony" Valcarcel (Co-Founder, Investor Relations), Mike Fehlauer Hayes (Co-Founder, helped create PAX and former VP of Penny Arcade), and me (Co-Founder, Director of Operations).

Origin

The prototype I built

Before Makko AI had a name, a CEO, or a team, I was building the prototype on my own. I wrote the first commit. I shipped the first working version. Tony Valcarcel joined later to help me build it out further. The two of us got it to a state that attracted Jeremy Bird (now CEO), Mike Fehlauer Hayes, and the rest of the founding team. The prototype became the company.

Most founder stories rewrite the early history once the team grows. This is mine: I started Makko AI as a one-person prototype. Tony came on to help build. Then the founding team came together around what we had built.

My scope at Makko AI

Four areas I own end-to-end

01

Keyword research and SEO strategy

I authored the SEO and AEO Master Playbook that governs every Makko AI article. Keyword research runs through Ahrefs with documented criteria: KD analysis, search intent classification, SERP feature mapping, and competitor gap analysis. I migrated the blog from a subdomain to the root domain, implemented schema markup across the site, and built the topic cluster planning system from scratch.

02

Backlink and authority building

I run the backlink profile end-to-end. Digital PR outreach, HARO and Qwoted pitching, broken-link reclamation, citation building, niche directory placements, and guest-post outreach. Every link tracked in Ahrefs and prioritized by referring-domain quality rather than raw count. Documented link velocity reported to founders monthly.

03

Content production with AI workflows

I have shipped 48 published blog articles against the playbook. Drafts run through Claude and Cursor with custom prompt libraries that I version like code. Every draft is human-reviewed before publishing. I also direct YouTube metadata, social content across eight platforms, and email lifecycle templates through Mailchimp and Resend.

04

Marketing operations and tooling

The content calendar lives on GitHub Pages. The UTM tracking infrastructure lives at go.makko.ai. The integration layer covers PostHog, Ahrefs, GSC, Bing Webmaster Tools, Ghost CMS, Gleam.io, Mailchimp, and Resend. I make the tool procurement calls and document the operating rules so the team is not relearning logistics every week.

05

Positioning and brand

I made the call to position Makko AI as art-first rather than game-builder-first based on the user signal that 70% of users come for the art pipeline. I co-developed the brand positioning, messaging hierarchy, and competitive framing that now governs every external communication. Brand is a strategic moat.

06

Director-level operations

Budget allocation across content, paid, and tooling. Vendor management for external video production. Quarterly OKR and KPI setting. Tool procurement decisions across the marketing stack. Hiring screens for content roles when we scale. Reporting cadence to the founding team. The unglamorous infrastructure that compounds when documented.

Decisions

Specific calls I made at Makko

Built the original Makko AI prototype

I started Makko AI as a one-person prototype. I wrote the first commit. Tony Valcarcel joined later to help me build it out. The prototype became the company.

Position Makko AI as art-first, not game-builder-first

User analytics showed 70% of incoming users came for the art generation pipeline. Repositioned external messaging, landing page hierarchy, and content calendar around art-first within a single week. Bounce rate dropped, time-on-page rose.

Led the rebrand from Snib.ai to Makko AI

Owned naming, positioning, messaging architecture, and the brand system. Maintained full product velocity during the transition. No pause in shipping, no team disruption.

Migrated the blog from subdomain to root domain

Subdomain blogs accrue authority separately from the main domain. With Makko at DR 12, splitting authority between two surfaces was the wrong trade. Consolidated to makko.ai/blog, redirected legacy URLs with 301s, centralized link equity on one domain.

Built the UTM registry at go.makko.ai before scaling distribution

Untracked links lose campaign attribution forever. Stood up a UTM short-link domain with a documented naming convention before publishing the first batch of cross-platform content. Every external link is now attributable to campaign, channel, and content piece.
